## About

I run **Fang's Workshop**, a one-person studio focused on designing and building games. I have over five years of experience across multiple engines, with a particular interest in gameplay systems, enemy AI, and server-authoritative multiplayer architecture. I care about games that are tight, readable, and fun to replay.

I am currently expanding my stack with **C#** and **Python**, and picking up **Construct 2** for rapid 2D prototyping.
